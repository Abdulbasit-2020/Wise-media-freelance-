About This Service

Design That Gives Your Story Its Identity

Your book deserves a presentation that communicates its message and appeals to its intended readers. WMF's Book Cover & Interior Design service supports authors who need professional visual presentation and organized interior formatting.

Whether you are preparing a new manuscript or refreshing an existing book, the design scope will be agreed before production begins.

Project Scope

What's Included and What's Not

Potential Deliverables

  • Book cover concept and design work, where selected.
  • Interior page layout and formatting support.
  • Design alignment with the agreed book specifications.
  • Final files in the formats agreed for the project.
  • Revisions according to the confirmed package.

Not Automatically Included

  • Manuscript proofreading or editing.
  • Book publishing or distribution fees.
  • ISBN purchase or third-party platform fees.
  • Printing and physical book production.
  • Additional design work outside the agreed scope.
These are scope guidelines, not a promise that every item is included in every package. WMF must confirm the exact deliverables before payment.

Before We Begin

What We Need From You

Providing the right information helps WMF understand your expectations and prepare the project correctly.

Manuscript

Your final or approved manuscript, preferably in an editable document format.

Book Information

Book title, author name, genre, subtitle and any other text that must appear on the cover.

Creative Direction

Your preferred style, reference designs, colour preferences and intended audience.

Book Specifications

Intended trim size, page count, publishing format and any available platform requirements.

Images and Assets

Any approved photographs, illustrations, logos or other assets you want considered.

Special Instructions

Any specific design requirements or instructions that WMF should consider.
