About the Service

Your Story Deserves a Visual Experience

Animated book content uses movement, visual storytelling and other creative elements to present a story in an engaging way.

What Is an Animated Book Series?

It is a visual storytelling project that adapts selected book content into animated scenes or episodes. The format and production scope depend on the author's goals and the agreed project brief.

Who Is It For?

Authors, writers and publishers who want to explore animated storytelling as an additional way to present their written content.

Creative Possibilities

Animated Series Features

Depending on the agreed project scope, an animated book series may include the following creative elements.

Story Adaptation

Selected book content can be adapted into a suitable visual storytelling format.

Character Presentation

Characters may be represented visually according to the agreed creative direction.

Animated Scenes

Scenes can be arranged to communicate important moments and ideas from the story.

Text and Dialogue

Selected dialogue, narration or text may be incorporated where required.

Audio Elements

Audio and sound elements may be included where part of the agreed package.

Final Video Delivery

Completed animated content will be delivered according to the agreed project specifications.

Project Options

Different Ways to Present Your Story

Single Animated Story

A standalone animated presentation based on an agreed section or concept from a book.

Multi-Episode Series

A project structured into multiple episodes. The number of episodes and production details must be agreed before work begins.
