About This Service

Give Your Book a Stronger Promotional Presence

Publishing a book is an important milestone, but reaching readers also requires thoughtful promotion. WMF offers book marketing and promotional support designed to help authors present their books across selected digital channels.

Depending on the agreed project scope, support may include social media creative, Goodreads campaign support, YouTube promotion and Meta advertising management.

Every campaign must have a clearly defined scope, budget and delivery plan. Marketing services do not guarantee sales, views, followers or advertising results.

Project Scope

What's Included and What's Not

Advertising Transparency

Understand Your Campaign Costs

Advertising budgets must never be hidden inside WMF's service fee.

WMF Service Fee

This is the amount charged by Wise Media Freelance for the agreed marketing work, campaign management or creative services.

Advertising Spend

This is the separate budget allocated to advertising platforms. The amount and payment arrangement must be agreed before a campaign starts.

Example only: If WMF charges a service fee and the client also approves an advertising budget, both amounts must appear as separate line items on the order summary. No example amount is being presented as an actual WMF price.
