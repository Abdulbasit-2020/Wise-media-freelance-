Client Support Policy

Our support guidelines explain how clients can communicate with Wise Media Freelance and receive assistance throughout their projects.

Draft policy — requires WMF approval: This document is a proposed support policy. WMF must review and approve its procedures, contact arrangements and response-time commitments before publishing it as a final policy.

1. Introduction

Wise Media Freelance (WMF) aims to provide professional assistance to clients who have questions about its services, orders, payments or ongoing projects.

This policy explains the intended support communication process and the information clients should provide when requesting assistance.

2. Available Support Channels

Clients should contact WMF through the official website Contact page or another written communication channel officially provided by WMF.

All support communication is handled in writing. WMF does not offer telephone calls, voice calls, video calls or call-booking services.

3. Areas of Support

Clients may contact WMF for assistance with:

  • General questions about available services.
  • Service descriptions and project requirements.
  • Order submission and order confirmation.
  • Payment verification enquiries.
  • Project progress and delivery questions.
  • Approved changes to an existing project.
  • Problems accessing project files or deliverables.
  • Technical problems with the website or client portal.
  • Complaints and service-related concerns.
  • Refund and cancellation enquiries.

4. Information to Include in a Support Request

To help WMF understand and investigate an enquiry, clients should include relevant information.

Recommended Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address registered with WMF.
  • Order reference, if an order exists.
  • Service or package involved.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Relevant screenshots or documents, if necessary.

Clients should avoid sending passwords, bank login details, card PINs or other sensitive authentication information through support messages.

5. Support During an Active Project

Clients with active projects may use the approved written communication channel to ask questions about their agreed project scope, progress, requirements or deliverables.

Requests that change the original scope may require a separate assessment and written approval before additional work begins.

WMF will communicate relevant project information according to the agreed service arrangement.

6. Response Times

WMF intends to review support enquiries and respond as reasonably practicable.

Actual response times may depend on enquiry volume, complexity, staff availability and whether additional information is required.

A specific support operating schedule and response-time commitment must be confirmed by WMF before publication.

7. Urgent Issues

Clients who experience an issue that prevents access to an active project or an important deliverable should clearly describe the problem when contacting WMF.

Where possible, clients should explain the immediate impact of the issue and provide relevant supporting information.

WMF will assess the issue and determine the appropriate next steps.

8. Complaints and Service Concerns

WMF welcomes written feedback from clients who believe that a service has not met the agreed requirements.

When submitting a complaint, clients should explain:

  • What happened.
  • Which agreed service or deliverable is involved.
  • Why they believe the issue needs attention.
  • What supporting information is available.
  • What resolution they are requesting, if known.

WMF will review the information and communicate its response through written communication.

9. Client Responsibilities

Clients can help WMF provide effective support by:

  • Providing accurate contact and order information.
  • Explaining issues clearly and respectfully.
  • Responding to reasonable requests for clarification.
  • Submitting required project materials on time.
  • Keeping copies of important project communications.
  • Protecting their account credentials and private information.

10. Website and Technical Issues

If a client experiences difficulty using the WMF website, submitting an order or accessing available website features, they should report the issue through the Contact page.

Clients should describe the affected page, the action they were attempting and any error message displayed.

WMF will investigate reported issues and take reasonable steps to address problems within its control.

11. Privacy and Confidentiality

Clients should only provide information reasonably necessary to resolve their enquiry.

WMF should handle personal information and project materials in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

Clients should not send confidential account credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary support messages.

12. Support Limitations

Support enquiries do not automatically authorise additional work outside the original approved project scope.

New services, substantial changes or additional deliverables may require a separate quotation, approval and payment arrangement.

WMF also cannot guarantee outcomes controlled by external publishers, advertising platforms, marketplaces or other third parties.

13. Policy Updates

WMF may update this policy when its support procedures or business requirements change.

The final published version should display its applicable effective date.

14. Contact WMF Support

For assistance, questions or complaints, visit the official WMF Contact page.

Include your order reference where applicable so that your enquiry can be directed to the appropriate support process.

Policy effective date: To be confirmed by WMF.

Policy status: Draft — pending review and approval.

How Can We Help?

Have a question about a service, order or ongoing project? Send WMF a written message through the Contact page.
