Refund & Cancellation Policy

Important information about cancellation requests, refund assessments and payment-related concerns at Wise Media Freelance.

Draft policy — requires approval: This document is a proposed policy for Wise Media Freelance. It must be reviewed and approved by WMF, including its refund rules and applicable legal requirements, before it is published as a final policy.

1. Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how Wise Media Freelance (WMF) intends to assess requests to cancel an order or obtain a refund for services purchased through its website.

By placing an order, clients should review the agreed service description, project scope, quotation and applicable terms before making payment.

2. Full Upfront Payment

WMF requires 100% upfront payment for an approved service order before work begins.

Payment must be successfully received and verified before the project is authorised to start. A payment screenshot, transfer notification or unverified transaction reference does not by itself confirm that payment has been received.

Clients should ensure that they understand and approve the agreed project scope and applicable price before completing payment.

3. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

All cancellation and refund requests must be submitted in writing through the official WMF contact channel.

To help WMF identify and assess the request, clients should provide:

  • Order or transaction reference, where available.
  • Client's full name.
  • Email address used when placing the order.
  • The service or package purchased.
  • A clear explanation of the cancellation or refund reason.
  • Relevant supporting information, where applicable.

Submitting a request does not automatically mean that a cancellation or refund has been approved.

4. How Requests Will Be Assessed

Each request will be reviewed based on the circumstances of the order and the applicable agreement.

The assessment may consider:

  • Whether work on the project has started.
  • The amount and nature of work already completed.
  • The original service description and agreed scope.
  • Any approved changes or additional work.
  • Third-party costs or commitments disclosed and approved in advance.
  • Relevant consumer protection rights and applicable law.
  • Any other terms agreed between WMF and the client.

WMF will communicate its decision after reviewing the available information.

5. Requests Before Work Begins

If a client requests cancellation before work has started, WMF will review the request and determine whether a full or partial refund is appropriate.

The assessment will take account of the agreed contract, any applicable commitments or costs, and the client's legal rights.

A request made before work begins does not automatically guarantee a full refund in every circumstance.

6. Requests After Work Has Started

Where work has already started, WMF will consider the actual work performed and the terms agreed before the project began.

The assessment may include completed work, project preparation, approved third-party services, agreed deliverables and any applicable legal rights.

Any refund decision will be based on the circumstances of the particular order. WMF will not treat every cancellation request as automatically refundable or automatically non-refundable.

7. Completed Projects

If the agreed service has been completed or the deliverables have been provided, a refund request will be assessed against the agreed scope, the work delivered, the contract and applicable law.

Clients who believe that a delivered service does not match the agreed scope should contact WMF in writing and explain the concern.

8. When WMF Cannot Deliver the Agreed Service

If WMF becomes unable to deliver an agreed service, it will communicate the situation to the affected client and work toward an appropriate resolution under the agreement and applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, a resolution may involve a revised delivery arrangement, another agreed solution or a refund where appropriate.

The specific resolution will be communicated to the client. No particular remedy is promised by this draft policy in advance of that assessment.

9. Third-Party Costs and Custom Services

Some projects may require external tools, platforms, subscriptions, production services or other third-party commitments.

Any such cost or commitment that WMF intends to charge to a client must be disclosed and approved before it is incurred on the client's behalf.

The written project agreement should explain how approved third-party expenses will be treated if an order is cancelled, including any non-recoverable expenses, subject to applicable law.

10. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, WMF intends to return the funds through the original payment method wherever reasonably practicable.

The time required for the funds to appear in a client's account may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution involved.

WMF will communicate relevant refund information after approval. Actual processing times may be outside WMF's direct control.

11. Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

Clients who have concerns about a payment or order are encouraged to contact WMF in writing so that the issue can be reviewed.

Clients retain any rights they may have to raise a payment dispute with their bank, card issuer or payment provider.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict rights provided by applicable law.

12. External Results and Refund Requests

WMF provides creative, publishing-related, marketing and other professional services within the scope agreed with each client.

WMF does not guarantee specific external outcomes, including:

  • A particular number of book sales or revenue.
  • Acceptance or approval by a publisher or platform.
  • Specific search rankings or visibility.
  • Advertising performance or campaign results.
  • Audience growth or commercial success.

The absence of a guaranteed external result does not, by itself, determine whether a refund is due. Refund requests will be assessed according to the agreed service, actual performance and applicable legal requirements.

13. Changes to This Policy

WMF may review and update this policy when its services, business procedures or legal obligations change.

Any final policy should state when the updated version takes effect. Applicable legal rights will remain unaffected.

14. Applicable Law

The interpretation and application of this policy will be subject to the laws and consumer protection requirements applicable to the transaction.

WMF must confirm its official business jurisdiction and obtain legal advice before inserting a specific governing-law or dispute-resolution provision.

15. Contacting WMF

For cancellation requests, refund enquiries or payment-related concerns, clients should contact WMF through the website's Contact page.

Please include your order reference and the information requested in this policy so that your enquiry can be reviewed.
