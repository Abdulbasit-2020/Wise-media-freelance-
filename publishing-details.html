Important Information

Understand Your Publishing Responsibilities

Publishing Accounts and Ownership

Before beginning a publishing project, the author and WMF should agree on account ownership, access permissions, copyright responsibilities, ISBN arrangements where applicable, platform fees, royalties and any third-party charges.

Publishing approval and platform decisions may depend on the publishing platform and its own requirements. No publication date or platform approval should be assumed until confirmed.
