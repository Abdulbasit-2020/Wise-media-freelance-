About the Service

Give Your Book a Visual Identity

A book trailer is a short promotional video designed to introduce a book and communicate its atmosphere, theme or central message.

What Is a Book Trailer?

A book trailer is similar to a movie trailer. It uses visuals, text, sound and storytelling elements to introduce a book to potential readers.

Why Choose a Book Trailer?

A trailer provides an additional promotional format that authors can use when presenting their books online, on social media and across suitable marketing channels.
