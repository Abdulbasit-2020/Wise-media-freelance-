Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Wise Media Freelance. Learn how information may be collected, used and protected.

Important notice: This is a draft policy for the WMF website. It must be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual business details, technologies, data practices and legal requirements before publication.

Effective date: To be confirmed

1. Introduction

Wise Media Freelance (referred to as "WMF", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of individuals who visit our website, enquire about our services or engage with our business.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored, shared and protected when interacting with our website and services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services used and the features implemented on our website, WMF may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity information: Name and other identifying information voluntarily provided by a client.
  • Contact information: Email address and other contact details voluntarily submitted through approved communication channels.
  • Project information: Project descriptions, service requirements, instructions and files provided for an agreed project.
  • Account information: Information associated with a future WMF client account, where account registration is available.
  • Payment information: Payment status, transaction references and confirmation records required to administer orders.
  • Technical information: Certain device, browser and website usage information may be processed by hosting or website technologies.

WMF does not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for the relevant enquiry, service or business operation.

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected when you:

  • Submit an enquiry through a website form.
  • Request information about a WMF service.
  • Place an order or submit project requirements.
  • Create a client account, when that feature becomes available.
  • Upload project materials through an approved portal.
  • Communicate with WMF through written channels.

Some website features are still under development. Information collection will depend on the features that are actually active.

4. How We Use Information

Where applicable and permitted by law, WMF may use collected information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Prepare project proposals and confirm project scope.
  • Manage client orders and project requirements.
  • Verify payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Provide project updates through written communication.
  • Manage client accounts and project files.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and administrative obligations.

5. Payment Information

WMF requires full upfront payment before work begins. Payment must be verified before an order is treated as paid and authorised to proceed.

Where third-party payment providers are used, payment processing may be subject to the provider's own privacy policy and terms.

WMF should only retain the payment information necessary for legitimate business records and verification. Full payment card numbers, security codes and account passwords should not be requested through ordinary website enquiry forms.

6. Project Files and Documents

Clients may need to provide manuscripts, images, design materials and other project documents.

Such materials should only be accessed by authorised personnel who need them to perform the agreed work. WMF intends to use appropriate access controls and secure storage when its client file portal is implemented.

Clients should avoid uploading unrelated sensitive personal information.

7. Firebase and Third-Party Technologies

WMF plans to use Firebase technologies for selected website functions, which may include authentication, database services and file storage.

When these features are activated, relevant information may be processed by the technology providers under their applicable service terms and privacy documentation.

The actual Firebase services, configurations, storage locations and data-processing arrangements must be confirmed before this policy is published.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if required for functionality, security, preferences or analytics.

Any optional analytics or advertising technologies should be disclosed and configured in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

9. Sharing of Information

WMF does not intend to sell clients' personal information.

Information may be shared where necessary with:

  • Technology providers supporting website operations.
  • Payment providers processing authorised transactions.
  • Authorised service personnel working on a project.
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary.
  • Public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Any sharing should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and subject to appropriate safeguards.

10. Data Security

WMF intends to use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include access restrictions, secure authentication, appropriate database rules, backups and secure file handling.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their account credentials and report suspected security issues promptly.

11. Data Retention

Personal information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting and business recordkeeping requirements.

Actual retention periods and deletion procedures must be established by WMF before the website's data systems go live.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a privacy complaint with the appropriate authority.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

13. Children's Privacy

WMF's website and services are not intentionally designed to collect children's personal information without the necessary legal authority and safeguards.

If WMF becomes aware that information has been collected improperly, appropriate steps should be taken in accordance with applicable law.

14. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. WMF does not control the privacy practices of those external providers.

Users should review the privacy policies of external websites before submitting personal information to them.

15. Changes to This Policy

WMF may update this Privacy Policy when its services, website technologies or legal obligations change.

The latest version should be published on this page together with its effective date.

16. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

If you have a question or request concerning this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please use the WMF Contact page.
