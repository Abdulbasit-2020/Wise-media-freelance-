Administrator Security

The production WMF administration system must be protected by authenticated administrator accounts and server-side permission checks.

  • Only authorised administrators may access administrative information.
  • Client records must be protected from unauthorised access.
  • Payment verification must be performed through trusted server-side processes.
  • Important administrative activities should be recorded in an audit log.
  • Administrator access should support strong authentication and appropriate permission levels.
  • Backups and recovery procedures must be established before production launch.
